HeatViper - Thermal Imaging Surveillance Drone

HeatViper is a military-grade thermal surveillance drone designed for long-range night patrol and zero-visibility operations. With precision thermal IR imaging and 5km detection range, it delivers crystal-clear heat signatures for accurate threat detection in complete darkness, fog, and smoke conditions. Ideal for border security, search and rescue, and covert reconnaissance.
