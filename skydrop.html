SkyDrop - GPS-Guided Precision Payload Drone

SkyDrop is a military-grade GPS-guided payload delivery drone engineered for precision operations in high-risk environments. With autonomous navigation, 5kg payload capacity, 10km range, and 60-minute flight endurance, it delivers supplies and payloads exactly where needed—keeping operators safe. Ideal for tactical resupply, emergency aid delivery, and precision strike missions.
