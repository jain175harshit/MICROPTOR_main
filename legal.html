Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and safeguard data

We collect only the data needed to operate our platforms, secure our infrastructure, and support mission readiness. Access is limited to authorized personnel, and we do not sell customer information.

What we collect

  • Account details and role-based access data
  • Operational logs for safety, compliance, and troubleshooting
  • Contact information for support and training

How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit and at rest for sensitive records
  • Least-privilege access controls and activity monitoring
  • Retention schedules with options to request correction or deletion

We use your data to deliver services, meet legal and safety requirements, and improve reliability. For questions or data requests, contact us via our contact page.
